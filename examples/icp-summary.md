# ICP summary: Examplia food producers

Tier 1: 246 companies for 2 people × about 40 accounts a month × 1 quarter.
The best fit is a bakery or dairy producer with 20–99 staff that has been trading for 8 years or more.
List: Examplia market map, food manufacturing, pulled 2026-10-12, 1,412 in-market companies. Evidence: path A, 36 customers matched (30 best, 6 of them held out).

*Made-up example. See [README.md](README.md).*

## Who fits
Size matters most: firms with 20–99 staff are 3.5–6.7× more common among the best customers than in the market. Bakery and dairy come next. Age adds a little on top of size. Region doesn't matter.

| Dimension | Max | Bands → points |
|---|---|---|
| Size | 40 | 1–9: 0 · 10–19: 15 · 20–49: 35 · 50–99: 40 · 100+: 30 |
| Activity | 30 | bakery or dairy: 30 · other food: 15 |
| Age | 15 | under 3: 0 · 3–7: 8 · 8+: 15 |
| Revenue | 15 | below €2m: 0 · €2m–30m: 15 · above: 10 |

Disqualified: fewer than 5 staff, sole traders. Full matrix: [icp-matrix.md](icp-matrix.md).

## Back-test
5 of the 6 held-out best customers are in the top 20% of the market by score, against 1 of 6 expected by chance. The miss is a 140-staff dairy group that signed through its smallest company (14 staff). The matrix scored the entity that signed, not the group.
Worst customers average a score of 58, against 91 for the best. Of 9 lost deals, 3 sit in tier 1: they fit, so something other than fit lost them.

## Decisions made
- "Best" = top half of customers by yearly revenue, customer for 2 years or more
- Capacity: 2 people × about 40 accounts a month × 1 quarter = about 240
- Disqualifiers: fewer than 5 staff, sole traders (both hard limits)
- Region left out and dairy not given extra points, against the user's first belief; the user agreed
- Tier lines: tier 1 at 85, tier 2 at 65. A minimum score of 40 was the user's choice: it removes 20 companies, all with 1–9 staff and under 3 years old

## Caveats
- Customers are described as they are today; 4 best customers have more than doubled their staff since they signed
- Meat processing is untested, not a poor fit
- Group companies: the matrix scores the legal entity, so a large group can hide behind a small entity
