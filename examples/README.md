# Examples

**Everything in this folder is made up.** "Examplia" (`XX`) is a fictional country, and every company, number and domain is invented. The files show the shape of the work, not real data.

The setting is the same as the illustrative example in step 4: a company sells production-planning software to food producers, has 39 customers, and two people who can work about 240 new accounts a quarter. The list being tiered is an Examplia market map.

- [icp-matrix.md](icp-matrix.md): the filled-in matrix from step 4
- [list-tiered-full.csv](list-tiered-full.csv): a few rows after scoring and tiering, with every `icp_` column
- [icp-summary.md](icp-summary.md): the one-page summary for the whole market the rows came from

The rows show the common cases: a current customer, a held-out customer the matrix found, a company with no filed revenue, a high score with low confidence kept out of tier 1, a lost deal, a worst customer, two disqualified companies, and a row outside the market.
